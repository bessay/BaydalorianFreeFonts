



Thank you for downloading this font! I hope you enjoy it as much as we had making it! This was a huge collaboration from many font makers, and was about 2 months in the making. Below I have all the font makers involved and which portion of the font everyone did, along with the proffie blade style.

 Thank you for supporting your fontmakers, and MTFBWY!
